About

I'm a junior at Rice University studying Mechanical Engineering but with a particular curiosity in exploring the intersection of mechanical design, embedded electronics, and whatever my mind is currently fixated on (right now it's how to make LLMs even more fun for me). That's likely why I'm really into rapid prototyping whether it be making a custom PCB with no previous knowledge in electrical engineering or outputting a full aerodynamics package in less than a month.

On a more professional note, I've worked at Atec Inc. on aerospace throttle interface tooling and at TAMOS Inc. on UHV heat chambers for quantum ion trapping systems. Outside of class, I play basketball, produce beats on my actual drum machine, and lead the aerodynamics team for Rice FSAE. Above all, my goal is to share my creative outlet of building things from scratch to others as I've found I have the most fun when not knowing what to expect from engineering.
