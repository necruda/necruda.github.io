About

I'm a junior at Rice University studying Mechanical Engineering with a minor focus in electrical systems and computational methods. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design, embedded electronics, and rapid prototyping — whether that's a custom 4-layer RF PCB for a rocket transponder, an aerodynamics package for a formula-style race car, or an LLM-driven desktop robot.

I've worked at Atec Inc. on aerospace throttle interface tooling and at TAMOS Inc. on UHV chambers for quantum ion trapping systems. Outside of class, I lead aerodynamics for Rice FSAE and avionics for ECLIPSE Rocketry.
